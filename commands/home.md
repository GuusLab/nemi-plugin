description: "Open Nemi: storage, the coming week, recent documents and shortcuts"
---

Open the user's Nemi home with `nemi_home`.

In a client with interactive views the result is shown as their home screen: storage, the next seven days on their Nemi calendars, recently edited documents, their workspaces and shortcuts. Do not repeat what it shows. Add at most two lines about what needs attention (something on today, storage above 90 percent), or nothing at all if nothing does.

In a client without views, summarise the result in a few lines under: Coming up, Recently edited, Storage, Workspaces.

If `nemi_home` is not offered, build the same picture from `workspace_list`, `storage_info`, `calendar_events` and `doc_list`.
