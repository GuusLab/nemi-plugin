---
description: "Catch up on what changed in my Nemi workspace and what needs me"
argument-hint: "[since when]"
---

Give the user a short catch-up on their Nemi workspace. Period: $ARGUMENTS (default: the last seven days).

Start with `nemi_home` when it is offered: one call that returns the workspaces, storage, the coming seven days and the documents edited last, and in a client with interactive views it shows the user their home while you read the rest. If it is not offered, read `calendar_events` and `storage_info` instead.

Then read, in parallel where possible: `calendar_events` for the coming seven days (skip it if `nemi_home` already gave you the week), `form_list` (forms with new responses), `doc_list` and `sheet_list` (recently changed), `upload_link_list` (files that arrived), `beam_list` (files beamed in), `share_list` (links expiring soon) and `storage_info`.

Write at most a dozen lines under these headings, leaving out any heading with nothing in it: Coming up, New responses, Files that arrived, Recently changed, Expiring soon, Storage. Finish with the one or two things that most need the user's attention. Change nothing.
