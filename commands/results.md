Summarise the responses to this Nemi Form: $ARGUMENTS

If no form was named, list recent forms with `form_list` and use the one with the newest responses, saying which one you picked.

Follow the run-forms skill. Use `form_responses` and quote its counts rather than counting rows. Lead with the number of responses, then the two or three findings that stand out, then open-text themes with one or two short quotes, then anything that needs action. Keep it short enough to read in a minute.
