argument-hint: "[today | tomorrow | this week | next week | a date range]"

Show the user's agenda from their Nemi calendars for: $ARGUMENTS

If nothing was given, show today and tomorrow.

Use `calendar_events` with the matching ISO window, resolved in the user's time zone. Group events by day in time order, with start and end times, title, and location or meeting link when present. Point out clashes and days with no break. End with one short line saying that calendars connected from Google, Outlook or ICS are not included. Follow the plan-with-calendar skill.
